Discover the Pacific with authentic local guides

Pacific Heritage Tours connects travellers with the people, stories and landscapes of the Pacific islands. From cultural village visits to coastal walks and traditional craft workshops, every experience is designed to be respectful, small-group and memorable.

Explore our tours or book your place today.

Why travel with us

Local expertise

Our guides are community members who share genuine knowledge of language, custom and place.

Small groups

We keep group sizes limited so every guest receives personal attention and the experience stays low-impact.

Cultural respect

We work directly with villages and cultural centres, ensuring benefits stay within the communities we visit.

Featured experiences

  • Village Cultural Day – traditional welcome, storytelling and shared meal
  • Coastal Heritage Walk – guided interpretation of landscape, plants and history
  • Craft & Weaving Workshop – hands-on session with local artisans

View all tours and programmes

Who we welcome

Our tours are suitable for independent travellers, couples, families with older children, and small groups of friends. We design experiences that support realistic visitor goals: learning about Pacific cultures, supporting local communities, and creating lasting memories.
