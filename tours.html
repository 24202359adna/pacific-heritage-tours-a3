Our Tours & Programmes

All experiences are led by local guides and keep group sizes small. Prices shown are indicative and may vary by season and group size. Contact us for current availability.

Day experiences

Village Cultural Day

A full-day immersion including a traditional welcome, storytelling session, village walk and shared meal prepared by community hosts.

  • Duration: approximately 6–7 hours
  • Group size: maximum 10 guests
  • Includes: guide, welcome ceremony, lunch, transport from meeting point

Coastal Heritage Walk

A guided walk along coastal and near-shore paths with interpretation of plants, fishing practices, historical sites and environmental change.

  • Duration: 3–4 hours
  • Group size: maximum 8 guests
  • Includes: local guide, light refreshments, interpretive materials

Craft & Weaving Workshop

Hands-on session with local artisans focusing on traditional weaving or carving techniques. Guests create a small piece to take home.

  • Duration: 2.5–3 hours
  • Group size: maximum 6 guests
  • Includes: materials, instruction, finished item
