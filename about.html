About Pacific Heritage Tours

Our story

Pacific Heritage Tours began as a small initiative to create meaningful visitor experiences that honour local knowledge and support community livelihoods. We partner with village leaders, cultural centres and independent guides who already work to keep traditions alive.

Rather than large-scale tourism, we focus on carefully designed day experiences and short programmes that allow guests to listen, learn and participate respectfully.

Our values

Respect

We follow local protocols and seek permission before sharing stories or visiting sites.

Reciprocity

Fair payment and long-term relationships are central to how we work with communities.

Learning

Every tour includes interpretation so guests leave with deeper understanding, not just photographs.

Low impact

Small groups, responsible waste practices and clear guidelines protect the places we visit.

Who we work with

Our network includes:

  • Village cultural committees and elders
  • Independent local guides and storytellers
  • Artisan cooperatives and craft practitioners
  • Community-based conservation groups

We do not operate large resorts or high-volume excursions. Our focus remains on quality of experience and genuine cultural exchange.

Who our tours are for

Pacific Heritage Tours is designed for travellers who want more than a surface-level visit. Typical guests include:

  • Independent travellers seeking authentic cultural insight
  • Couples and small groups of friends
  • Families with older children who can participate thoughtfully
  • Educators and students on study tours
